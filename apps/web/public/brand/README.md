# Sparrow Brand Mark

Concept A (top left) from the round-two exploration was selected for the final
mark. The hand-authored vector keeps its compact right-facing sparrow, simple
eye, short tail, and broad folded-paper wing. The standalone mark is transparent;
the icon and favicon place the same silhouette on a light sage tile. SVG is the
canonical source.

The source mark's alpha contour measures 452 by 337 pixels (1.341:1) within the
concept sheet. The canonical paths use a Bézier fit through that extracted contour.
Body and wing remain separate components, preserving
the complete transparent channel across the wing's upper and lower edges.

The complete image-generation prompt and source provenance are preserved in
[`docs/brand-exploration/round-two/prompt.md`](../../../../../docs/brand-exploration/round-two/prompt.md).

Originality is a design goal, not a trademark-clearance guarantee.
