**Re-arm after an interruption.** When your human interrupts the session (Esc / Ctrl-C), your process tree usually goes with it — including the background `await` that is your only wake path. (What exactly Codex tears down on an interrupt is **not verified**; treat it as gone until you have checked.) The listener stamps the heartbeat `killed:<signal>` (or `stopped:SIGINT`) as it dies, so the hooks catch it for you: the next prompt tells you to re-arm, and the Stop hook refuses to let that turn end in silence. Re-arm first, then carry on.
